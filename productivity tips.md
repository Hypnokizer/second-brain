# "9-1-1" and the Soft Dash
When separating the numbers in 9-1-1, sometimes they will word wrap at the end of typed lines. In order to prevent this from happening, there is a character known as a soft hyphen. It looks very much like a regular hyphen. When typing, hold down `Ctrl + Shift` and press the hyphen key. This will create a soft hyphen and keep the word from wrapping at the end of a line.


# Pivot Tables in Excel

When you would like to see totals, averages, or other aggregate functions, but do not want to use Access, pivot tables could be the answer.
