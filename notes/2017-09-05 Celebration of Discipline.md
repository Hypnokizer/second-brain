**Psalm 42**: (v7) deep calls to deep; God has put in us a desire to know Him intimately. When we know God in a deep way, we can be the answer to a hollow world!

Somewhere you've heard a call to deeper, fuller living. You've become weary of frothy experiences and shallow teaching. Every once and awhile you catch glimpses of something *more* than you have known...

Sin works its way through the body until it becomes ingrained. Isaiah 57:20 says *"The wicket are like the tossing sea; for it cannot rest, and its waters toss up mire and dirt"* It is a natural product. No wonder we feel trapped!

Our regular method of dealing with sin is a frontal attack. We rely on our willpower and determination. Willpower has no defense against the unguarded moment. *"Out of the overflow of the heart the mouth speaks"*

This path does not produce change, but places us where it can occur! The goal is to shape us into the image of Jesus Christ. To know the mechanics does not mean we are practicing the Disciplines.

We cannot free and purify our own heart by exerting our own will

God has given us the Disciplines of the spiritual life as a means of receiving His grace. They allow us to place ourselves before God so He can transform us. A farmer is helpless to grow grain; all he can do is provide the right conditions for the growing of grain.

*"The path of disciplined grace"*; grace is free, but not cheap.

The grace of God is unearned and unearnable, but if we ever expect to grow in grace, we must pay the price of a consciously chose course of action which involves both individual and group life.

...no longer a tiring need to hide our inner selves from others...
