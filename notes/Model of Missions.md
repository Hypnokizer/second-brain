Hoosiers: A Model of Missions

I watched *Hoosiers* recently.

People hired him because they saw the results of his work elsewhere. They didn't see the effort and work leading up to those results. Most based their entire judgment on his results and/or numbers. He was the only one in town expected to give a public accounting of all his mistakes &amp; failures (church/town hall meeting).

Gene Hackman leveraged his position to reach others (Shooter). He was hired to teach a program (basketball). Along the way, he reached people. He taught them how to "do it" for themselves.

Hackman was transparent. He showed his weaknesses. He let himself get kicked out of a game so Shooter could step up and lead (discipleship). He admitted to his past mistakes.
