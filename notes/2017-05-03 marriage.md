# Day 1

Be the person you'd like to be married to. If you need more love & understanding, give more love & understanding to your spouse.

# Day 2

Nobody is perfect. Don't let the world find out about your spouse's imperfections via you. (Prov 31:11)

# Day 3

Make it a point to give your spouse the best of you: the best of your touch, the best of your (good) attitude, the best of your laughter, the best of your words, etc. Don't always bring them what's left of you after the world has drained you.

# Day 4

Be intentional. Each day look for a way to bless your spouse. A thankful, giving heart will repulse blame discontent. ("Outdo one another in showing honor")

# Day 5

Believe the best about your spouse, even when they don't communicate well. Sometimes it's hard to find the right words.

# Day 6

Greet your spouse with a kiss. Positive touch releases endorphins so you associate your spouse with pleasant feelings. Bonus: You can't say anything regrettable when your lips are occupied (Song of Solomon 1:21)

# Day 7

Life affects all of us. No one remains the same. We change physically, act less romantic, etc. Take time to find the two people who fell in love and reconnect. ("Rejoice in the wife of your youth." - Prov 5:18)
