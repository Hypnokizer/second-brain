(January 12, 2007)�It took them long enough to figure this out, but researchers say procrastination in society is getting worse.

After ten years of research, a Canadian industrial psychologist has found that not only is procrastination on the rise, it makes people poorer, fatter and unhappier.

That project was only supposed to take five years.

The study in this month's Psychological Bulletin finds that 26 percent of Americans think of themselves as a chronic procrastinator.

That's compared to about five percent in 1978.

Part of the problem, one researcher says, is that things like iPods, cell phones and online videos make procrastination effortless.
