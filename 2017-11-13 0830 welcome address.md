**2018 is the 50th anniversary of 911**

Computers, when introduced, were a disruption. So was TDD/TTY, wireless, VoIP, and text. Every exciting thing we've done, we've retrofitted to 1968!

We spend a lot of tiem controlling the call *(voice)* and have a hard time digesting data, much less *more* data! Voice is important, but not always accurate. We need to use data to help triage the calls. New hires are more data-centric than voice.

Our industry *fears* data. We try to fit everything into a voice mold.
