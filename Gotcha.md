**GOTCHA!**

**Objective:**  Don’t get got.

**How to play:** On Monday night, you will secretly receive the name of a fellow Eng-Games prospect. Your goal is to shoot him/her with a water gun. Once you shoot your target, he/she will give you the name of the person they were after. This is your new target. **The game begins Tuesday, October 4****th** **at 12:00 am.** Players still alive on Saturday October 15th, at 12:00 am will be re-spawned and a new target list will be generated. The game will re-commence 24 hours after the re-spawn date, on Sunday October 16th at 12:00am.

**Goal:** Be the last person alive in GOTCHA! and get a spot on Engineering Games Team Concordia!

**Rules:**

1. If you shoot the person chasing you before he/she shoots you, you have one (1) hour of immunity.

2. “Misleading” people by telling false names of targets is allowed, but you _must_ reveal your true target once shot.

3. Your target is immune if he/she is out of bounds. Out-of-bound areas are as follows:

· Classrooms

· Labs

· _Inside_ your target’s home (unless you have been invited)

· 30 feet from any Concordia Security kiosk

· Reggie’s/Java U/Tim Horton’s/Chartwells or any business operating on campus

· ECA office, Eng-Games office, the Cage, ECA lounge, 7th floor conference rooms

4. Kills must be made with _water_ using a _water gun_ or water propelling mechanism. Buckets, water bottles, etc. are not permitted.

5. Once you kill someone, you must notify the appropriate Eng-Games exec. (Lists will be updated either online, at the ECA office, or Eng-Games office.)

6. You must kill at least one (1) person before each re-spawn date, or you will be eliminated.

Questions? Email enggames@eca.concordia.ca
