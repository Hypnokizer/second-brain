# Lubbock Facts

These facts were taken from a presentation made by Mayor Dan Pope at a SHRM luncheon on 2018-06-28.

The U.S. Census indicates Lubbock's population is 254,565.

54,000 of Lubbock residents attend TTU, LCU, SPC, or Wayland. These schools graduate more than 13,000 students annually.

Lubbock's unemployment rate is 2.8%, which is lower than the U.S. average of 4.1% and less than the State average of 3.7%.

## Job Growth Stats

* 5,500 jobs have been created over the past (2) years
* 9,500 jobs have been created over the past (5) years
* 16,000 jobs have been created over the last (10)

## Did You Know?

* Traffic builds, installs, and maintains about 100,000 signs every year.
* Health inspectors conducted 4,830 inspections in 2017.
* The police department received 167,911 emergency calls and 260,380 non-emergency calls.
* The public library has 11,191 digital books available for checkout.
* The fire department maintains 6,318 fire hydrants.
* The Lubbock average commute time is 15.5 minutes. The national average is 25 minutes.
* Lubbock maintains 618 miles of alleys.
* 2,103 potholes were filled in FY 16-17.
