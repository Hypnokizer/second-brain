*Diffusion is the process in which an innovation is communicated throughout the members of a social system.*

### The Innovators (venturesome)

* obsessed with innovations
* ability to understand and apply complex technical knowledge
* OK with a high degee of uncertainty
* have more resources than most other categories

### The Early Adopters (respected)

* look to early adopters for advice and info about an innovation
* serve as role models for many other members of the social system
* trigger the critical mass when they adopt and innovate
* must make judicious innovations/decisions; risk can be somewhat high, but can also be mitigated
* give the idea the "stamp of approval"

### The Early Majority (deliberate)

* represents roughly 1/3 of the community
* adopts the idea just before the average member of the system
* interacts frequently with peers, but seldom holds positions of opinion leadership
* *"Be not the first by which the new is tried, nor the last to lay the old aside."*

## The Late Majority (skeptical)

* 1/3 of the system
### The Laggards (traditional)

* possess almost no opinion leadership; most local vision
* isolates in social networks
* suspicions of innovations and change
* usually subject to economic pressures

## Adoption Factors

* relative advantage of innovation; the greater the advantage, the more rapid the adoption
* Do we need to change our value system to adopt this new technology? *(at one point, we did not trust telematics)*
* newer ideas that are simpler to understand are adopted more rapidly
* observability is the degree to which results of an innovation are visible to others *(solar energy tend to be deployed in clusters; when we see it, we believe it!)*

*How do you eat an elephant? One bite at a time...*

## Five Steps to the Adoption of an Innovation

These steps occur over time and the speed of each step dictates the adoption rate.

1. knowledge
2. persuasion
3. decision
4. implementation
5. confirmation

Change agents have a high level of education, certification, etc. They are often perceived as a deviant from the social system and may have a low credibility by the early majority.
